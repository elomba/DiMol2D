Technical specifications:
  • Two dimensional systems
  • Classical molecular dynamics simulation
  • The system is periodically replicated in the two directions of the bidimensional space
  • Numerical integration of the equations of movement
  • ''Two-step velocity Verlet'' algorithm
  • RATTLE algorithm for diatomic molecules
  • Temperature Control: ''ad-hoc''thermostate ( velocity scaling in each integration step)

Interaction Potentials and external fields:
  • Lennard-Jones 12-6, (truncated and shifted)
  • Lennard-Jones 10-4 for wall-particle interactions (truncated and shifted), truncated at the potential minimum for horizontal wall-atom interactions ( puramely repulsive potential).
  • Uniform gravitatory field (along the y-axis) with g =0.02 (simulation internal units).
  • Symmetric interactions (in mixtures):


Systems
  • Pure substance
  • Binary mixtures (2 components)
  • Ternary mixtures (3 components)
  • Diatomics (component A)
  • Heteronuclear diatomics (component A). In this case the A molecule is formed by B and C atoms, with attractions between like atoms and repulsion between unlike ones.

IMPORTANT
The phase diagrams provided correspond to the bulk atomic systems (no confinement, no gravity) with default interactions, i.e. Lennard-Jones truncated at 2.5 for like interactions and 1.12 for unlike interactions (pure repulsion).
